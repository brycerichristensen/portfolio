HLS Mission Analysis & Visualization

During the summer of 2026, I worked as a Guidance, Navigation, and Mission Analysis Intern at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center (MSFC) in Huntsville, Alabama. My work focused on supporting the Human Landing System (HLS) program through lunar landing visualization tools, automated telemetry pipelines, and incorporating TRN in-the-loop capabilities.

Terrain-Relative Navigation & Lunar Visualization

Precision landing on the lunar surface requires real-time optical matching between onboard camera telemetry and high-resolution lunar digital elevation models (DEMs). To support Hardware-in-the-Loop (HWIL) testing, I developed visualization tools for the Human Landing System to evaluate Terrain-Relative Navigation (TRN) performance.

These simulations mapped simulated descent trajectories against shaded lunar terrain models, allowing engineers to visualize feature-tracking confidence, shadowing effects, and optical sensor alignment during powered descent and hazard avoidance phases.

Automated Starship Telemetry Pipelines

Analyzing high-frequency flight data across complex test campaigns requires robust, repeatable data processing workflows. I engineered automated python-based telemetry and flight visualization pipelines designed to process dynamic dataset feeds from SpaceX’s Starship test flights.

The pipeline parses raw sensor logs, synchronizes spatial state vectors, and renders interactive 3D trajectory profiles. This streamlined post-flight analysis, enabling rapid extraction of attitude rates, burn durations, and trajectory deviations against baseline mission models.

Methalox RCS Thruster Sizing & Thermal Management

Deep-space attitude control for lunar landers demands compact, high-efficiency propulsive reaction control systems (RCS). I performed detailed sizing and preliminary design evaluations for a liquid methane and liquid oxygen (methalox) RCS thruster block.

The evaluation covered injector geometry trade studies, spark torch igniter integration, propellant feed system line sizing, and regenerative/film cooling thermal management calculations to ensure stable impulse bit performance across extended vacuum operations.
